# Q4 quality rubric, provenance and measurement plan (T049)

Status: **pre-review record**, written 2026-10-30 for issue #65. This is the rubric the Q4 art review
(T057) will use; it is not the review and it does not claim a modern look. **No numeric resource or
frame budget is set here.** The numbers come from the T056 measurements, and until those measurements
exist the only honest state is `blocked` (see `content/manifest/budget-rows.json`).

The rubric is deliberately separable from classic parity. A frame that matches the 2011 OpenGL renderer
is not a Q4 pass; Q4 asks whether the hybrid path's materials, lighting, shadows, indirect light and
atmosphere look deliberate, and whether the HUD and authored content stay identifiable while they do.

A review row may only cite an artifact that exists in the repository. A missing artifact is `BLOCKED`
with the reason, never `PASS`.

## 2. Hero-set provenance and permissions

Every reviewed hero asset must satisfy all of the following before its image is judged:

- The provenance record (`content/hero/<name>/provenance.json`) names the authoring input, records the
  file SHA-256 and answers `retail_text_consulted`.
- The licence is **CC0-1.0** and a `LICENSE` file sits beside the assets. A fan pack, retail asset or
  third-party texture may never be relabelled as a hero (`specs/009-rhi-hd-content/boundary.md` C-04).
- The asset is authored, not extracted: `inputs` names a generator or painting source, not a pack.
- Nothing in the hero directory exceeds the C-01b size cap; nothing derived, staged or binary is
  tracked beyond the authored PNG/EXR allowed by the contract.
- A material in the Q4 set has both a `.mtr` and a `.mat.json` sidecar, every channel carries a state
  (`authored` / `derived:<method>` / `unavailable`), and the sidecar's normal handedness is declared.

**Current record.** `hero_dielectric` meets this: three 512×512 RGBA PNGs generated by
`content/tools/generators/dielectric.py`, `retail_text_consulted: no`, `licence: CC0-1.0`, hashes in
`provenance.json`. The other six cells now have project-owned generators, `LICENSE`, `provenance.json`
with `retail_text_consulted: no`, and complete sidecars; their packages build. They still cannot be
reviewed as images because no Q4 frame exists. Their cell-specific limits are recorded, not hidden:
`skinned_character` authors a surface but no rigged mesh/animation; `emissive` authors an SDR mask but
HDR radiance above 1.0 is blocked on the C-10 format decision; `transmissive_glass` authors
baseColor.alpha and roughness while opacity/refraction stay with spec 004; `large_environment` assembles
4096×4096 from committed 2048 tiles but its residency row stays blocked on T056.

## 3. Scene identities

A review capture must name a scene identity, not just a screenshot. The fields below are fixed before
the capture so a second person can repeat it. Extents are choices for the measurement, not budgets.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `scene` | stable name, unique per capture |
| `renderExtent` / `displayExtent` | both stated (for example 1280×720 render, 2560×1440 display) |
| `camera` | position, direction and FOV, or the camera path and its key frames |
| `content` | material list from the Q4 set, plus prop/geometry identity |
| `lighting` | direct lights (kind, colour temperature or linear radiance, range), shadow casters, probe/reflection source, atmosphere settings |
| `motion` | static, rigid, skinned or viewmodel; frame numbers for a moving capture |
| `overlay` | HUD identity and whether it is display-resolution |
| `backend` / `adapter` / `driver` | required by spec 004 FR-018 and spec 009 FR-013 |

The four scene categories the review must cover are:

1. **static opaque surfaces** — dielectric and metal, one direct key plus ambient/probe;
2. **alpha-tested and emissive surfaces** — a grating/foliage cutout and an HDR emissive sign;
3. **moving content** — a rigid prop and a skinned character, reviewed for material/identity stability
   under motion;
4. **transmissive and large environment** — glass/transmission and the large environment surface with
   the residency test surface.

All four categories are **BLOCKED on a renderer frame**. They are no longer blocked on authored content:
the five cells named above exist and build. Four dependencies remain separate from content and are
recorded in the sidecars: a rigged mesh and animation for `skinned_character`, an HDR representation for
`emissive`, the transmission model for `transmissive_glass`, and the T056 residency measurement for
`large_environment`.

## 4. Rubric dimensions

Each dimension is judged `accept`, `reject` or `not applicable`, with the artifact and the reviewer's
reason recorded in `review-q4.md`. "Looks fine" is not a verdict; the reviewer names the observation.

### 4.1 Materials

- **Accept**: base colour, roughness/normal and metalness relationships are plausible for the stated
  material; a legacy specular map was not reinterpreted as roughness; an HDR emissive keeps detail above
  1.0 rather than clipping to a flat white; a normal map's lighting direction is consistent (no inverted
  green channel, no handedness flip).
- **Reject**: a dielectric reads as metal (or the reverse), roughness is uniform where the sidecar
  declares variation, a legacy specular highlight is presented as a PBR roughness result, an authored
  channel is missing and a default was silently substituted, or a normal map shows an axis swap.
- **Evidence**: the sidecar, the package manifest and the capture; plus the T050 fixture result for the
  conversion/fallback rule.

### 4.2 Lighting and shadows

- **Accept**: direct light falls off and shadows land where geometry implies; shadow bias does not detach
  a contact shadow or produce visible acne; an unsupported legacy stage is routed to the compatibility
  path rather than shaded as an opaque surface; a light that declares `noShadows` casts none.
- **Reject**: a shadow floats or is offset from its caster, a translucent/glass surface receives the
  opaque direct term, a legacy stage is silently promoted, or the direct term is clamped so HDR
  highlights cannot exist.
- **Evidence**: `tests/renderer/lighting_test.cpp` for the numeric rule; the capture for the image.

### 4.3 Indirect light, reflections and atmosphere

- **Accept**: indirect light lifts shadowed surfaces without flattening contact; a reflection points to
  plausible environment content (or a documented ground/horizon fallback below the horizon); fog is
  depth-consistent and does not tint the HUD; an atmospheric HDR colour keeps its range.
- **Reject**: a below-horizon reflection is black, a reflection is mirror-sharp at a rough surface,
  fog brightens distant geometry inconsistently, negative/NaN radiance reaches the frame, or the HUD is
  fogged or resampled with the scene.
- **Evidence**: `tests/renderer/indirect_atmosphere_test.cpp` for the arithmetic and artifact cases; the
  T086 ordering check for HUD separation; the capture for the image.

### 4.4 Darkness and readability

- **Accept**: the darkest reviewed area still shows surface separation and navigable silhouettes; the
  brightest emissive is not a clipped blob that destroys nearby detail; dark scenes are not used to hide
  material or shadow defects.
- **Reject**: detail is lost in black, the only visible feature is a bloom-like white patch, or the
  reviewer cannot tell two adjacent materials apart without moving the camera.
- **Evidence**: the capture and a stated exposure/tonemap configuration. No numeric contrast threshold is
  invented here; T056 records the measured values first.

### 4.5 Identity

- **Accept**: the reviewed content is recognisably the authored material/silhouette across the four
  categories; a skinned character keeps its material and silhouette through motion.
- **Reject**: a metal hero reads as plastic, a character's material swims under skinning, or the large
  environment surface changes appearance with camera distance beyond a declared mip transition.
- **Evidence**: the capture naming the material and scene identity; provenance for the source.

### 4.6 Motion

- **Accept**: rigid and skinned motion is stable; history/reprojection does not leave a visible ghost on
  a moving object while the background is static; a cut or teleport resets rather than smears.
- **Reject**: ghosting on a rigid prop, smeared skin weights, or a moving object whose material changes
  between frames. The Q3 fixtures cover the mechanism; this dimension judges the image.
- **Evidence**: a moving capture with frame numbers and the Q3 evidence for the mechanism.

### 4.7 HUD / overlay

- **Accept**: the HUD is composed after reconstruction at display resolution, texel-sharp, and is not
  fogged, upscaled or fed into motion history; in-world GUI stays at render extent and follows the scene.
- **Reject**: a blurred HUD, a HUD that moves with the scene's reprojection, or a HUD drawn before the
  scene.
- **Evidence**: `tests/renderer/reconstruction_test.cpp` (T086 off-path ordering) and the capture.

## 5. Measurement plan (feeds T056)

T056 measures the accepted hero set and the four scene categories on named hardware, then sets the
numeric budgets here or records why they stay unset. The plan is fixed now; the numbers are not.

For every capture record:

| Field | How it is obtained |
|---|---|
| backend, adapter name, vendor/device ids (when reported), driver version | the backend's adapter info and the OS driver query; a zero/unreported id is recorded as unreported, never as a real zero |
| render/display extent, quality settings, upscale mode | the scene identity, plus `PlanReconstruction`'s mode |
| committed texture bytes and peak resident bytes | `ResidencyPolicy` accounting and the package manifest; the policy is the same one T054 tests |
| upload bytes per frame and deferred uploads | `ResidencyTick` counters |
| frame time (min/median/max) and frame count | the capture harness's timestamps; state the sampling window |
| memory over budget / deferred releases | residency tick counters; a deferred release is reported, not hidden |
| accepted/rejected examples | the reviewer's rubric rows, with artifact paths |

Rules:

1. A measurement missing any of backend, adapter, driver, extent, scene identity or command is
   incomplete and cannot set a budget.
2. Thresholds are recorded **after** the measurement, in the same PR that adds the evidence, before the
   review verdict. A threshold added after a rejection is not a threshold.
3. The budget rows in `content/manifest/budget-rows.json` move from `blocked:<reason>` to `recorded`
   only with an artifact path. A bare number is a spec 009 SC-009 violation.
4. No measurement in this plan is a vendor claim; the hardware is whatever the host actually reports.

## 6. What is not claimed

- T056 is `BLOCKED` on completeness, not absence. A real static frame and a partial moving capture
  exist for the one authored cell in the run (`opaque_dielectric`) on Intel(R) Graphics and WARP; the
  run predates the six-cell content merge at `127ab39`, and the other six cells plus the
  alpha/emissive, skinned-character and transmissive/large-environment categories are `NOT RUN`.
  All seven content cells now have an authored source, a CC0 licence, provenance, a sidecar and a
  buildable package, but the full seven-cell/four-category capture set and measurement do not exist;
  this is not a visual pass.
- T057 is `NOT RUN`: an independent reviewer has not recorded a verdict on this rubric or on T056's
  captures. Captures now exist for one cell and two partial categories, but they are not the
  seven-cell/four-category set the review needs; the absence of that full set, not the absence of any
  capture, is the reason.
- The T086 provider-specific display-stage position is `NOT RUN`: no authorized provider exists (T060),
  and the check requires T062 execution.
- Numeric budgets remain unset. That is the recorded state, not an omission.
